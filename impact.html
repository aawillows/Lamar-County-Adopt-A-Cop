What We Do

More than one project. One continuing mission.

Support That Has Grown With Our Community

Since 2016, Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop has grown from an effort focused on protecting local law enforcement into a broader mission supporting law enforcement officers, first responders, their families, and the communities they serve.

Protective & Safety Equipment

Over the years, the organization has helped place hundreds of pieces of protective and safety equipment into the hands of local law enforcement and first responders, including ballistic vests, ballistic helmets, and other resources agencies and responders need to do their jobs more safely.

Our impact is not one donation or one project. It is years of responding to needs, large and small, across Lamar County.

Supporting the People Behind the Uniform

First Responder & Family Assistance

Helping first responders and their families facing off-duty illness, injury, hardship, or unexpected circumstances.

Wellness, Education & Peer Support

Supporting resources and partnerships that strengthen resilience, wellness, education, peer support, and long-term well-being.

Community & Public Safety

Supporting programs such as the Reno Police Department Special Heroes Unit and other initiatives that strengthen accessibility, inclusion, and public safety.

Appreciation & Morale

Reminding the people behind the badge, radio, ambulance, and fire engine that their service is seen and valued.

Many Needs. Many Ways to Serve.

Protective equipment, community initiatives, sensory support, hardship assistance, responder appreciation, and partnerships are all parts of a mission that adapts to real local needs.

Uplift for Life

Uplift for Life is organized by a dedicated community group that selects Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop as a beneficiary of its annual fundraising efforts. Their support helps strengthen our ability to assist local first responders, families, and community needs.
