OUR IMPACTGET INVOLVED

Protecting Responders. Supporting Families. Strengthening Community.

Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop supports law enforcement officers, first responders, their families, and the communities they serve through protective and safety equipment, wellness and educational resources, assistance during off-duty illness, injury, or hardship, and support for community needs and public safety initiatives.

OUR MISSION IN ACTION

Nearly a Decade of Showing Up

Protective Equipment

Helping provide ballistic vests, helmets, safety equipment, and other resources that help local responders do their jobs more safely.

Responder & Family Support

Standing with first responders and their families during off-duty illness, injury, hardship, and other unexpected circumstances.

Community Support

Partnering with local agencies and organizations on programs, disaster response, Special Heroes, sensory support, appreciation efforts, and other community needs.

Our photographs tell this story too. Historical and current images will be presented throughout the site in their original proportions, with photographer credits and watermarks preserved.

MORE THAN A DONATION

Built on Relationships

Our impact has never been about a single project. It is the result of years of partnerships with first responders, families, businesses, volunteers, and community members who continue to step forward when there is a need.
