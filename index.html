Protecting Responders. Supporting Families. Strengthening Community.

Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop supports law enforcement officers, first responders, their families, and the communities they serve through protective and safety equipment, wellness and educational resources, assistance during off-duty illness, injury, or hardship, and support for community and public safety initiatives.

YEARS OF SERVICE

More Than One Project

From ballistic vests, helmets, and safety equipment to first-responder assistance, community programs, disaster relief, and partnerships, our story is built from years of responding when a need arises.

Some projects are large. Others may touch only one family, one responder, or one child. Each is part of the same mission: showing up for the people who serve and the community we call home.
