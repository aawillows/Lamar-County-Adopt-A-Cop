Supporting Those Who Serve

Providing equipment, resources, assistance, and support to law enforcement, first responders, their families, and our Lamar County community.

OUR IMPACT

Protecting Responders. Supporting Families. Strengthening Community.

Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop is organized for charitable purposes to support law enforcement officers, first responders, and the communities they serve. We provide safety equipment, wellness and educational resources, assistance to first responders and their families experiencing off-duty illness, injury, or hardship, and support for community needs and public safety initiatives.
