About Us

Our story, our mission, and the people carrying it forward.

Built to Back Those Who Serve

Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop began in 2016 following the tragic ambush of Dallas police officers. Following the lead of Brandi Leber with Hopkins County Adopt-A-Cop, Amanda Willows brought the idea home to Lamar County. What began as an effort to show support for local law enforcement quickly grew into a lasting commitment to help protect and support those who serve our communities.

From those beginnings, the organization has provided hundreds of pieces of protective and safety equipment, including ballistic vests, ballistic helmets, and other resources for local law enforcement and first responders.

Today, our work also supports first responders and their families during off-duty illness, injury, and hardship; wellness and educational resources; agency and community partnerships; and local needs where our support can make a meaningful difference.

Our Purpose Today

Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop is organized for charitable purposes to support law enforcement officers, first responders, and the communities they serve. The organization provides safety equipment, wellness and educational resources, assistance to first responders and their families experiencing off-duty illness, injury, or hardship, and support for community needs and public safety initiatives.
