Flag Program

Patriotism with a purpose.

A Community Tradition That Gives Back

The Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop Flag Program provides a meaningful way for residents and businesses to display the American flag while supporting our mission. Volunteers place and retrieve flags for participating locations around designated patriotic holidays.

Funds raised through the program help Lamar County Adopt-A-Cop continue providing equipment, assistance, resources, and community support for law enforcement officers, first responders, and their families.

Flag Program details are being updated. Current enrollment, service area, holiday schedule, and contribution information will be posted here as they are confirmed.
